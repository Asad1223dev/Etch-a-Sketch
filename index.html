Grid
Instructions:
Follow the instructions below:
  • Click on the Color Picker box to select the color.
  • Click the Hover button to draw by hovering over cells.
  • Click the Hover button again to turn it off.
  • Click on the Clear the Grid button to clear the grid.
  • Click on the Make New Grid button to create your own grid.
  • Remember to choose a grid size from 1 to 64.
  • To clear a single cell, double click it with the left mouse button.
